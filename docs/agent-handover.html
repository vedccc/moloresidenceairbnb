One note before the questions — about breakfast orders.

The knowledge base said that if a guest calls after 8pm, the AI should take their breakfast order and let reception know. We've taken that line out, because the AI has no way to do it — sending messages or emails to your staff isn't part of what was built, so “I'll pass it on to reception” would have been a promise with nothing behind it.

What it can do is tell the guest anything that's in the knowledge base, and put them through to a person. So for a late breakfast order it can already say “you can order through the SmartHotel app, or on the form at reception” — or offer to transfer the call. If you'd like it to say something specific in that situation, just add it to the knowledge base and it will.
